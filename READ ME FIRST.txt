Then reboot and hard-refresh (Cmd-Shift-R).

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. RIGHT SIDE NO LONGER STACKS.
   * Master Stock List: was one full-width row per ingredient. Now a
     4-across card grid - name, on-hand chip, pack/price, cost per
     recipe unit, supplier - the same card as the mock.
   * Tasks: the seven days were seven full-width cards stacked down
     the page. Now two per row, so the week reads across.

2. SIDEBAR SUB-HEADINGS NO LONGER COVERED.
   "DAILY RUNNING" and "OWNER TOOLS" had padding but no real height,
   so the nav pill below rode up over the text. They now have an
   explicit line-height and a minimum block height.

3. SIZE AND FONT CLOSER TO THE MOCK.
   Body copy 0.92rem and captions 0.83rem (Streamlit's defaults ran
   noticeably larger), h3 down to 1.05rem, cards on sand with an 18px
   radius, nested cards in cream.

4. The bright green completion notice is now the design system's sage,
   which stops it fighting the warm palette.
